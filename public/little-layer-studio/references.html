For curious makers

Halloween, with links.

Here are MakerWorld reference models for the six Halloween ideas. They are alternatives, not the files used for the pictures. The website pictures are original digital mockups and do not have exact MakerWorld downloads.

These links were found in indexed MakerWorld listings. Full model pages and their current licences have not all been accessible for verification. Check each creator’s commercial terms, dimensions, print requirements and permitted modifications before using a file. Free download does not automatically mean permission to sell prints.

01 / Pumpkin Patch

Kawaii Mini Pumpkin

A small Halloween pumpkin collection to explore. Faces and shapes differ from our three mockup pumpkins; check the listing’s actual designs.

View the MakerWorld listing ↗

02 / Boo Buddies

Cute Ghost

A friendly ghost-style alternative, not the exact pair shown. If lighting is suggested in the listing, use appropriate LEDs only. Never use a flame against printed plastic.

View the MakerWorld listing ↗

03 / Batty Little Bunch

Halloween Bat Ornament

A bat ornament reference for a hanging display. Confirm its thickness, attachment and size before planning a set of four.

View the MakerWorld listing ↗

04 / The Boo Crew

Fun, Fast Halloween Garland

A ghost, pumpkin and bat garland alternative. The motif shapes and assembly differ from our concept image.

View the MakerWorld listing ↗

05 / Say It With BOO!

BOO Sign for Halloween

A BOO display-sign reference. It is not the exact lettering, base or scale shown in the mockup.

View the MakerWorld listing ↗

06 / Your Name, Pumpkin

Pumpkin Name Tag

A pumpkin tag starting point. Check the creator’s instructions for adding a name in Bambu Studio and confirm modification rights.

View the MakerWorld listing ↗

Model and image permissions are separate. Before selling, use photos of the actual finished prints and confirm applicable product-safety requirements. Hanging cords and small decorations must be kept away from young children.
