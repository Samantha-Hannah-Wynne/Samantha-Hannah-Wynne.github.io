The person behind the layers

Hi, I’m Samantha.
Student. Maker.
Curious by default.

I’m a computer science student, and Little Layer Studio is my 3D-printing side hobby here in Dublin. It’s a place to explore what happens when a little technical curiosity meets a lot of colour.

I’m developing the first collection, from personalised name pieces to cheerful seasonal decorations. This site is a look at those ideas as they take shape — not a finished-product shop just yet.

Got a colour combination, a name or a little gift idea in mind? I’d love to hear it.

A few little answers

Before you say hello.

Can I order something now?

Not yet. I’m collecting interest and developing the designs. You can ask about a concept, but sending an enquiry does not place an order or reserve a price. I’ll confirm the final product, price and timing before any future order.

Are these photos of your prints?

No. All product pictures on this site are original digital mockups. They show an idea’s style, not the exact finish, dimensions or geometry of a printed item. Finished-print photos will replace them as the range develops.

Can I choose names and colours?

That’s the plan for the personalised collection. Tell me your preferred name and colour combination, and I’ll check what’s possible. Font support, size and final options need confirming.

Are the playful designs ready for children?

They are concept previews only. Any toy offered for sale will need the applicable Irish/EU toy-safety work, documentation and marking completed first. An age warning alone is not a substitute for that work.

Do you offer collection or delivery?

I’m based in Dublin. Collection, delivery areas, charges and timing are not set yet; they’ll be discussed and confirmed before any future order. There is no advertised walk-in shop.
