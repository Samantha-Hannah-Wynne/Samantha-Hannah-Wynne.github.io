    A few little answers

    Before you say hello.

    Can I order something new?

    It could be possible! Send me your idea and I’ll look into it first to see whether it’s something I can print. If it’s a good fit, we can chat about the design, price and timing before confirming an order. An enquiry isn’t a commitment to buy.

    How does the bundle calculator work?

    Add any of the 24 priced design concepts to your bundle, then adjust quantities to see an estimated item total. A quantity of one means the unit shown on the card, such as one pair or one set of four, not one piece from that set. The calculator adds the listed indicative prices without applying bundle discounts. Delivery, custom changes, final pricing and availability need confirming with me. You can send your selections as a WhatsApp enquiry; nothing is ordered or paid for on this site.

    Are these photos of your prints?

    The Already printed gallery contains my own photos of finished prints. The hero pictures and design collections are separately labelled digital mockups, not finished-print photos. Those concepts show an idea’s style rather than an exact printed product. Photos of past projects don’t imply that a design is available to order.

    Can I choose names and colours?

    That’s the plan for the personalised collection. Tell me your preferred name and colour combination, and I’ll check what’s possible. Font support, size and final options need confirming.

    Are the playful designs ready for children?

    They are concept previews only. Any toy offered for sale will need the applicable Irish/EU toy-safety work, documentation and marking completed first. An age warning alone is not a substitute for that work.

    Do you offer collection or delivery?

    Yes! You can collect by arrangement. Just reach out to me personally and I’ll share the collection address and agree a time with you. Delivery is also available in Dublin; message me to confirm your area, any delivery charge and timing before ordering.
