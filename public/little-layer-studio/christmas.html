For a very merry maker

Christmas, with links.

These MakerWorld listings are starting points for the six Christmas ideas. They are alternatives, not the exact files used for the original concept illustrations. Every Christmas picture on the collection cards is an original digital mockup, not a finished-print photograph.

A model being free to download does not mean its physical prints may be sold. Check the live licence and creator’s current commercial terms before accepting any request. Some listings offer commercial rights only through a paid creator membership. Also confirm dimensions, assembly, print settings and safety for the intended use.

Customisable Stocking Ornament

A personalised stocking ornament and gift-tag reference. The indexed listing says commercial rights may be offered through the creator’s membership; verify the current terms before selling.
